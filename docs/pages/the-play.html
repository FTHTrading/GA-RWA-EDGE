1 · The policy window — the honest four-quadrant read

"Take advantage of the administration" only works if you know which doors are open, which are closing, and when.

OPEN · PERMANENT

Opportunity Zones

OBBBA made OZ 2.0 permanent with a deliberate rural tilt — 30% basis step-up (vs. the historical 10%) and a 50% substantial-improvement threshold. New zone designations effective January 1, 2027. This is the policy the current administration wants used at scale — aligning with it is alignment, not arbitrage.

OPEN · FRIENDLIEST EVER

Tokenization & digital assets

GENIUS Act (stablecoins) signed into law; CLARITY Act advancing on market structure; SEC in an actively pro-tokenization posture (Project Crypto era). The regulatory risk that froze this industry for a decade is at its lowest point since 2013. Compliant-by-construction stacks like ERC-3643 are exactly what the new regime rewards.

OPEN · FAST-TRACKED

AI & data-center buildout

Federal posture is maximum acceleration: grid fast-tracking, federal-land siting, national-priority framing. Hyperscalers face 3-5 year queues for gigawatt campuses. Sub-5 MW modular rural pods bypass the queue entirely and land inside the exurban ring where inference demand is going.

CLOSING · SPRINT

Clean-energy tax credits

OBBBA phased out solar/wind ITC/PTC on a firm calendar: begin-construction safe harbor lapsed July 4, 2026; new-start solar must be placed in service by December 31, 2027 to earn any credit. Gigawatt projects can't make that date. Modular pods with on-site solar can — 90-day assembly is the arbitrage. Storage §48E, geothermal, hydro, nuclear, and 45Q carbon capture survive on longer timelines (2033 phaseout for §48E storage).

The strategic read The administration killed the subsidy era of clean energy but kept the asset era open — OZs, tokenization rails, and AI-compute demand are all pro-build. The play is speed: small solar that reaches placed-in-service by end-2027 captures the closing solar ITC; everything else anchors on hosting revenue and environmental attributes that don't depend on federal subsidy at all. See Government Programs for the full matrix with named administrators.

2 · The environmental asset layer — what gets added to the stack

Tier-1 attributes

RECs / SRECs

Every MWh of on-site renewable generation mints a certificate through a metered oracle pipeline: revenue-grade meter → signed attestation (AttestationVerifier) → 1 REC/MWh → reconciled to a registry serial (M-RETS / NAR / PJM-GATS). Georgia honesty: Georgia has no SREC compliance market — GA RECs sell bilaterally / voluntarily (often to the hosting customer itself). Pods sited in PJM states (VA, PA, OH, NJ, MD) earn compliance-market SRECs at real prices. Tier-1 / Class-I status (new-build renewables) is what compliance buyers must have.

Registry-anchored

Carbon credits

Voluntary-market credits (Verra VCS, Gold Standard, Puro for engineered removals) from: rural land reforestation / soil projects on QROF acreage, methane-capture power, biochar, and 45Q-eligible carbon capture. The oracle + ReserveProofAnchor stack is a native MRV (measurement / reporting / verification) engine — the exact credibility layer the post-scandal carbon market now demands (ICVCM / CCP-grade or don't bother). Rule: mint against registry serials only; retire on-chain with serial visible. No registry, no token.

Attribute premium

Green compute

The buyer of all of it already sits inside the pod: AI hosting customers need verified clean-compute claims (24/7 matching, carbon-neutral inference). A pod that meters its solar, mints its RECs, retires its credits, and proves all of it on-chain sells "verified green compute" at a premium — the environmental attributes aren't a side business, they're margin on the hosting contract.

Closing-window sprint

ITC + tax equity

Pod-scale solar placed in service by December 31, 2027 still earns the ITC — financed by a tax-equity partner via a standard 99/1 → 5/95 partnership-flip structure with a deterministic on-chain allocation ledger. Storage §48E ITC survives to 2033 — batteries pair with every pod regardless. After 2027, the environmental layer runs subsidy-free on RECs + carbon + premium hosting.
