2 · The issuer

The issuer is a single-purpose bankruptcy-remote limited liability company formed under [Delaware / Wyoming series] law. Its sole business is to own the physical assets and revenue contracts for a single edge data-center site. The initial site is the 5 MW Atlanta-market facility operating under a 4.9¢/kWh fixed-rate PPA in a deregulated gas zone.

2.1 Manager and role separation

The SPE is managed by [Sponsor Manager LLC] which is an affiliate of the sponsor. The SPE is separately administered by LD Capital LLC under a Master Services Agreement for QROF administration, servicing, and waterfall operation. Unykorn LLC provides technology rails (smart-contract configuration, attestation infrastructure, on-chain compliance) under a separate services agreement. FTH Trading distributes the securities via a BD-of-record partnership until FTH's own BD is approved.

2.2 Structural separateness covenants

  • The SPE will conduct no other business than owning and operating the identified site.
  • The SPE will maintain separate books, records, and bank accounts.
  • The SPE will not commingle assets with any affiliate.
  • The SPE will file separate tax returns and pay its own liabilities.
  • Independent director / independent manager appointment required per senior lender covenants.

3 · The offering

The issuer is offering up to $4,000,000 of tokenized Preferred Equity Interests to verified accredited investors under Regulation D Rule 506(c). Interests are held indirectly through the QROF Fund LP structure so that participating investors qualify for the OZ 2.0 tax benefits (subject to tract certification and individual investor eligibility).

3.1 Security description

  • Token standard: ERC-3643 (permissioned security token)
  • Transfer restrictions: Enforced on-chain by IdentityRegistry, ModularCompliance, Rule144LockupModule, CountryRestrictModule, and MaxHoldersModule
  • Rights: 8% cumulative preferred return; pro-rata share of residual distributions per waterfall; 30% share of upside on liquidity event after preferred return catch-up and sponsor promote hurdle
  • Voting: Non-voting except for major transactions (sale of substantially all assets, dissolution, amendment of LLC agreement in a manner adverse to preferred holders)

6 · Distribution waterfall

Distributions run deterministically through the on-chain CMBSWaterfall contract. The priority ordering is:

Priority 1  Utility / PPA payment    ← non-negotiable
Priority 2  Property tax + insurance escrow
Priority 3  Senior debt service (DSCRTrigger monitored)
Priority 4  Unykorn + LD Capital platform fees
Priority 5  Equipment lender debt service (parallel to Priority 3 if pari passu)
Priority 6  Preferred equity return (8% cumulative)
Priority 7  Return of investor capital pro rata
Priority 8  Sponsor promote (20% of residual)
Priority 9  Residual to common equity

Priority 1 is Priority 1 because a missed utility payment triggers PPA termination, which would collapse the deal thesis. DSCR at Priority 3 is tested net of Priority 1 utility charges.

7 · Tax considerations

7.1 QROF benefits (subject to tract certification and individual eligibility)

  • Investor cap-gain rolled into the QROF within 180 days is deferred until earlier of (a) taxable disposition or (b) December 31, 2032 (or such other date as OBBBA sets).
  • At year 5 of continuous hold, the investor receives a 30% basis step-up on the deferred gain (rural QROF tier), reducing taxable amount at inclusion.
  • At year 10 of continuous hold, appreciation on the QROF interest itself is generally excluded from tax via basis reset to fair market value, subject to OZ 2.0 30-year FMV election rules.

7.2 Depreciation pass-through

The Site SPE will pass through MACRS 5-year (with 100% bonus) depreciation on qualifying equipment (immersion systems, IT load, cooling, land improvements at 15-year). The bonus deduction is allocated to the taking partner subject to the partnership operating agreement. Investors should consult their own tax advisor regarding basis, passive activity, and section 469 implications.

7.3 What's NOT included

The base case does not include solar / wind ITC §48E — that begin-construction safe harbor lapsed July 4, 2026. Storage-only ITC and 45Q carbon capture credits remain available but are not modeled in the base case.

7.4 State tax conformity

State tax conformity with federal QOZ / QROF rules varies. Investors should consult their state tax advisor. Georgia generally conforms.

8 · Investor eligibility

8.1 Accredited investor verification

Under Regulation D Rule 506(c), an accredited investor is:

  • A natural person with individual net worth (or joint with spouse) exceeding $1M excluding primary residence, OR
  • A natural person with individual income exceeding $200k (or joint with spouse exceeding $300k) in each of the two most recent years with reasonable expectation of same in current year, OR
  • Holder of certain financial licenses (Series 7, 65, or 82) in good standing, OR
  • Various entity types with $5M+ assets or all owners qualifying as accredited, OR
  • Family offices with $5M+ assets under management

Verification will be conducted through Persona, Parallel Markets, or equivalent third-party verification service before any subscription is accepted. Self-certification is not accepted.

8.2 Non-U.S. persons (Reg S)

Non-U.S. persons may subscribe under Regulation S subject to a separate memorandum addendum. Reg S sales are subject to distribution compliance period restrictions and offshore transaction requirements.

9 · Subscription mechanics

  1. Access request · Prospective investor requests access via the accredited investor portal. Persona / Parallel Markets accreditation verification initiated.
  2. Review PPM · Verified investor reviews definitive PPM (this template becomes definitive after counsel review and after all pre-mainnet blockers clear).
  3. Subscription documents · Investor executes Subscription Agreement and Investor Questionnaire. Provides KYC / AML documentation. Provides tax lot information if using QROF cap-gain rollover.
  4. Wire to escrow · Investor wires subscription funds to a bank / trust escrow account controlled by an independent escrow agent. Funds do NOT go to Unykorn, FTH Trading, or LD Capital operating accounts.
  5. Compliance gates · IdentityRegistry maps investor wallet to verified identity. AMLTravelRuleGate screens the wallet. Accreditation claim posted on-chain.
  6. Token minting · At closing, tokens mint from Site SPE to investor's designated wallet address via CustodyAdapter 4-eyes flow (proposer ≠ approver, velocity caps enforced upstream).
  7. Custody · Investor may hold tokens at BitGo Bank & Trust, Anchorage, Fireblocks, or a compliant self-custody address. Uncustodied wallets subject to additional AML review.
  8. Reporting · Investor receives quarterly distributions statement, annual K-1 (for pass-through partners), and on-chain-verifiable audit reports.

10 · Custody and settlement

Subscription funds are held in bank / trust escrow — never in a platform operating account. This is an inviolable constraint. Investors sending funds to any wallet or account labeled "Unykorn," "FTH Trading," "LD Capital," or "platform" should treat that as a fraud red flag and stop the wire.

On-chain settlement of the tokenized security is atomic: at closing, the escrow releases fiat to the Site SPE bank account and the SPE mints tokens to the investor. If the investor's wallet fails a final compliance re-check at that moment, tokens are not minted and funds are returned per escrow instructions.

11 · Risk factors

An investment in tokenized Site SPE Preferred Equity Interests involves substantial risk. Investors should be able to bear the loss of the entire investment. Key risk categories:

11.1 Deal-specific risks

  • PPA counterparty and assignability risk: The PPA may not be cleanly assignable, may have unfavorable term length, or the utility may not accept novation. Any of these can materially reduce senior LTV or block the deal entirely.
  • Existing 721 miners: Existing miner contracts at the site may not be terminable or assumable on acceptable terms. Buyout costs may exceed the $2M provision in the use-of-proceeds.
  • Rural QROF designation: The target census tract is not certified as rural under the 2027 OZ 2.0 map until Q4 2026. Failure to qualify reverts the fund to standard QOF (10% step-up), reducing investor tax benefit by roughly $200k on a $4M block.
  • AI hosting counterparty risk: Contracted hosting revenue depends on a specific offtake counterparty. Concentration risk on a single tenant (like the Applied Digital / CoreWeave pattern) reduces resilience.

11.2 Structural risks

  • Illiquidity: Interests are subject to Rule 144 restrictions and OZ 10-year hold discipline. No public trading market exists at the time of this template. Secondary trading via partner ATS (Stage 1) or LDX (Stage 3) provides limited liquidity.
  • Regulatory framework risk: SEC, FINRA, IRS (QROF), and state securities laws may change. OZ 2.0 rules are subject to Treasury guidance not yet issued.
  • Technology risk: Smart contracts have not undergone independent security audit at this template stage. Pre-mainnet blockers include dual audits before any value moves on chain.

11.3 Investor-specific risks

  • Not all investors will qualify for QROF cap-gain rollover treatment; consult your own tax advisor.
  • State tax conformity varies.
  • Alternative Minimum Tax (AMT), Net Investment Income Tax (NIIT), and other individual tax items may apply.

  • Semiannual: CPA-attested QROF 90% asset test filing
  • Annual: K-1 for pass-through partners, audited financial statements, on-chain-referenced Reserve Proof Anchor summary

All reports are hash-anchored to the on-chain ReserveProofAnchor so that the report content is verifiable against the reported hash after the fact. This prevents ex-post rewriting of investor communications.

13 · Liquidity and exit

13.1 Year 1-5

No planned liquidity. Rule 144 restricted stock rules apply. Discretionary transfer to another verified accredited investor may be permitted subject to IdentityRegistry approval.

13.2 Year 5-10

QROF basis step-up locks at year 5. Discretionary secondary transfer window opens. Partner ATS (Securitize Markets, tZERO-class) may support limited resale subject to compliance gates.

13.3 Year 10+

Full OZ 10-year hold benefits vest. Sale of Site SPE or fund-level exit possible. LDX venue (if operational by Stage 3) supports on-chain secondary trading. Rated securitization exit possible if seasoned portfolio has grown to 3-5 sites.
