Funding Sources · Honest Map by Lifecycle Stage

Where the money actually comes from — separating who originates a raw single-asset deal from who levers, refinances, or exits a seasoned book. Two verticals with different answers: CRE hospitality (M Helen) vs. AI compute (Atlanta OZ).

The most common failure mode in RWA planning is treating a Centrifuge, Maple, or Grove Basin as an origination lender. They aren't. They price seasoned, cash-flowing, already-tokenized paper. So "who funds the RWA" splits into two very different questions: (1) who writes the first check into origination and (2) who levers or refinances the seasoned book. This page maps both — with 2026 H2 named counterparties and the honest constraint for each vertical.

The framing correction

Origination and levering are not the same conversation Protocols like Centrifuge, Maple, Clearpool, and Grove Basin are Stage-2/3 refinancing rails. None of them writes the first check into a raw single-asset deal like M Helen or a pre-construction Atlanta compute site. They price seasoned, cash-flowing, already-tokenized paper — meaning 12+ months of clean attested cash flow, ideally pooled across multiple assets. Stage-1 origination is still a TradFi conversation: CMBS, bridge, private credit, Reg D 506(c), Reg S, and QROF equity. On-chain rails plug in later — either as refinance takeout (Stage 2-3) or as parallel investor liquidity (Morpho/Fluid on tokenized equity).

The Grove clarification, because it's load-bearing

The July 2026 $500M Grove × Galaxy facility often gets cited as "on-chain RWA finance." It isn't. That facility is crypto-collateralized institutional credit: Grove is the warehouse lender, Galaxy Digital originates the loans, and the loans are overcollateralized with BTC and ETH — not real estate, not compute assets. Collateral is held at qualified custodians Anchorage Digital and BitGo. The facility is denominated in USDS, the Sky-ecosystem stablecoin.

Grove's real-world-asset-facing product is Basin, but the deployed capital in the headline transaction is digital-asset collateral. "Plug M Helen into Grove" is a category error today. The useful signal from that transaction is the custody rail: BitGo is already on the approved-custodian list for institutional-scale on-chain credit — which is exactly the rail LD Capital / Unykorn already use. Grove-class facilities become reachable at Stage 3 once a seasoned pooled book exists, not at origination.

On-chain role for CRE — EXIT LIQUIDITY, not origination

Once M Helen equity or senior debt is tokenized under ERC-3643 (T-REX), the token holder gets on-chain liquidity via:

  • Morpho Blue — isolated market for ERC-3643 tokens as collateral / USDC borrow. ~$6.8B TVL, 200+ isolated markets. Same rails Apollo's sACRED runs on (Securitize + Gauntlet cleared the legal template).
  • Fluid — clean investor-liquidity primitive.
  • Aave Horizon RWA vaults — layer-1 protocol legitimacy for permissioned-RWA collateral.
  • Euler v2 — modular alternative to Morpho Blue.

Path to Stage-2 mid-book scaling (Centrifuge / Maple pools): 12+ months of clean attested cash flow across more than one asset. One hotel is not poolable. Aggregating 3-5 seasoned hospitality assets into a pool is the origination work that unlocks Centrifuge/Maple takeout.

The honest constraint

On-chain capital is a slice, not the whole. Tokenized RWAs reached only ~$28.9B in May 2026, against AI infrastructure spend that hyperscalers plan to push past $5 trillion by 2030. What USD.AI / Trad.Fi / Framework fund is deployed, metered GPU / hardware with credible attestation. They are not a diligence platform and they do not fund greenfield sites. The path is:

  1. Site is powered on with a PPA locked (Barak: already done at 4.9¢/kWh, 5 MW, 721 miners live)
  2. Oracle attestation of GPU deployment + metered telemetry emitting on-chain (Unykorn compliance rail — already deployed)
  3. Cash flow wrapped into ERC-3643 senior notes with waterfall (LD Capital tokenization architecture — already deployed)
  4. Non-recourse pledge to USD.AI (or Trad.Fi/W3 / Framework) against tokenized GPU cash flow → drawdown into USDC / USDS
  5. USDC deployed into next site expansion — recycling capital without touching TradFi
