Bucket 1 — Federal Opportunity Zone & cap-gains

ProgramBenefitEligibility / thresholdStatus
QROF (Qualified Rural Opportunity Fund) — 30% basis step-up 30% step-up in basis after 5-year hold on cap-gains rolled into a rural QROF (vs. 10% for standard QOF). Triple the near-term shelter. Investment must be into a QROF classified as rural under OBBBA. Rural test: no city >50k population AND no urbanized-area contiguity. Generally effective for investments beginning 2027 per current IRS guidance — early 2026 investments may still take the 10% step-up. STABLE (2027+)
QROF / OZ 2.0 — 50% substantial-improvement threshold (rural) Substantial-improvement requirement dropped from 100% to 50% of original adjusted basis for existing property in rural QOZs. Critical for buildings and container-adjacent infrastructure. Rural OZ tract only. Applies to acquired existing property. STABLE — effective July 4, 2025
QOF / QROF — 10-year cap-gains-to-zero Federal capital gains on the QOF/QROF investment appreciation → $0 after 10-year hold. Any Qualified Opportunity Fund investment held ≥ 10 years, disposed by the fund investor. STABLE — made permanent under OBBBA
OZ cap-gains deferral Deferral of the original cap-gains liability until 2026 tax year (recognition event) if rolled into a QOF within 180 days. Standard OZ eligible gain (short-term, long-term, §1231, §1250 recapture components — check with CPA). STABLE
Practical read for Barak & Metro Atlanta exurban sites Every GA-RWA-EDGE target site sits inside a QOZ tract confirmed against the OBBBA rural test. The 50% substantial-improvement threshold is active right now for acquired existing property (Barak's containers + infrastructure). The 30% basis step-up applies to investments beginning 2027, which means the intro-offer / Reg D 506(c) subscription cadence should time the largest cap-gains-rollover LPs to close after January 1, 2027 for the maximum step-up. Investments closing in 2026 still access the 10% step-up plus the 50% substantial-improvement threshold — meaningful but not the full stack.

Storage ITC §48E — battery / BESS / geothermal / hydro / nuclear 30% investment tax credit on standalone energy storage (battery), geothermal, hydro, and nuclear. Facility placed in service. Prevailing-wage / apprenticeship requirements for full 30%. Bonus adders (10% each) for domestic content and energy communities. STABLE — through 2033, phaseout begins 2034

§179D EECBD — Energy Efficient Commercial Building Deduction Up to $5.81 per square foot deduction for energy-efficient lighting, HVAC, and envelope in commercial buildings. Construction must begin on or before June 30, 2026. Projects broken ground by that date remain eligible even if placed in service later. Must reduce energy use vs. the applicable ASHRAE efficiency standard. TIME-SENSITIVE — construction start ≤ 2026-06-30
§45Q — Carbon Capture Credit Per-ton tax credit for CO₂ captured and sequestered or utilized (values vary by disposition path; $85/ton for saline storage under IRA). Facility captures CO₂; storage or utilization documented per §45Q rules. Direct-pay / transferability available. STABLE
MACRS + 100% Bonus Depreciation 100% bonus depreciation on qualifying property in year one. Made permanent for property placed in service after 2025-01-19 under OBBBA. Restores year-one write-off previously scheduled to phase down. MACRS 5-year for compute / GPU / servers. 5-year or 7-year for cooling and racks depending on classification. Vehicles & certain fixtures shorter class lives. STABLE — permanent
§179 Property Expensing Immediate expensing of qualifying property in year one. 2026 cap: $2.56M; phase-out threshold $4.09M. Real property improvements (roofs, HVAC, fire, security) qualify at the same cap. STABLE

Bucket 3 — Federal rural & SBA

ProgramBenefitEligibility / thresholdStatus
USDA Business & Industry (B&I) Guaranteed Loan Up to $25M per project. Federal guarantee 80% (loans ≥$5M) or 85% (loans <$5M) against principal loss. Rural area (population <50k, non-urbanized). Approved lender originates; USDA guarantees. Broad-use — real estate, working capital, equipment, refinance. STABLE
USDA REDLG (Rural Economic Development Loan & Grant) 0% interest RUS pass-through loans up to $2M; grants up to $300k for project development. Sub-lender is a USDA rural utilities service borrower (electric or telecom co-op). Rural project. Subordinate layering under senior debt. STABLE
USDA REAP (Rural Energy for America Program) Grants + guaranteed loans for renewable energy systems and energy-efficiency improvements in rural areas. Small business or agricultural producer in rural area. Renewable energy, energy efficiency, storage. STABLE (verify current NOFA)
SBA 504 Subordinate CDC-funded fixed-rate debt up to $5.5M at rates typically 100-200 bps below senior CMBS. Standard stack 50% senior / 40% SBA 504 / 10% sponsor. Owner-occupied (≥ 51% owner-occupancy). Fits M Helen boutique / independent-flag hospitality; less relevant to compute SPEs. STABLE
SBA 7(a) Guaranteed working-capital / general business loans up to $5M with 75-85% federal guarantee. Small business per SBA size standards. Broad-use. STABLE

Bucket 4 — Georgia state incentives

ProgramBenefitEligibility / thresholdStatus
Georgia Data Center Sales & Use Tax Exemption (HB 696, extended by HB 1291) Full sales-and-use tax exemption on high-tech data center equipment purchases (servers, compute, cooling, networking). Minimum aggregate investment and job-creation thresholds based on county population. County population <50k: lower threshold; larger counties: higher thresholds. Certification through Georgia Department of Revenue. POLICY RISK — Currently valid through 2033 per HB 1291 extension. However SB 410 (2026) passed the Georgia Senate to repeal the exemption; House consideration ongoing. Model both retention and repeal scenarios.
Georgia Job Tax Credit (JTC) Per-job tax credit varies by county tier. Tier 1 counties (least developed) up to $4,000/job/year for 5 years. Tier 4 counties up to $1,250/job/year. Minimum job creation depending on tier (2 jobs Tier 1, 5-25 jobs Tier 2-4). Rural counties heavily favored. STABLE
Georgia Investment Tax Credit 1-8% credit on qualified capital investment in eligible business enterprises. Eligible business classes (manufacturing, telecom, warehousing, R&D). Tier-dependent rate. STABLE
Georgia Rural Zone Tax Credit $2,000 job credit + $4,000 investment credit (per year, up to 5 years) for revitalization of qualified rural zones. Designated Rural Zones (state-designated downtowns/redevelopment areas). Investment or job creation within zone. STABLE
OneGeorgia Authority — Equity Fund, EDGE Fund, Regional Economic Business Assistance (REBA) Grants and low-interest financing for infrastructure and workforce in rural Georgia. Rural county application through OneGeorgia Authority. Partnership with Georgia Department of Community Affairs. STABLE (application-driven)
Georgia Quality Jobs Tax Credit $2,500 to $5,000 per job for 5 years for creating 50+ new "quality jobs" above county wage floor. Wage floor 110% of county average. Various eligible industries. STABLE
Georgia Tourism Development Act (M Helen relevant) Sales-tax rebate to help finance tourism attraction development. Approved tourism-attraction project. Application through Georgia Department of Community Affairs. STABLE (application-driven)

Utility & power — Georgia specifics

Correcting a common misread — electricity vs. gas in Georgia Georgia's natural gas market is deregulated in the Atlanta Gas Light (AGL) service territory: delivery through the regulated pipe, but the molecule itself is bought from competing certified marketers. Georgia's electricity market is not deregulated the way gas is — Georgia Power is a regulated monopoly under the Territorial Electric Service Act, with a specific large-load carve-out (>900 kW). The PSC ordered a base-rate freeze on Georgia Power through 2028 (July 31, 2025 order), with data-center customers assuming risk for 3 years thereafter. Fuel-cost adjustments still pass through to ratepayers.
Program / StructureBenefitEligibility / thresholdStatus
Georgia Power Base-Rate Freeze through 2028 Base rates for Georgia Power customers frozen through 2028 per Georgia PSC order (July 31, 2025). Applies to Georgia Power customers. Fuel-cost adjustments still flow to customers separately. Rate case expected 2028. STABLE through 2028
Territorial Act Large-Load Carve-out (>900 kW) New premises with initial connected load above 900 kW may choose their electricity supplier at initial connection. New premise (not a legacy connection reassigned). Certified retail supplier available. No PSC restriction on premise class. Site-specific PSC / supplier confirmation required. STABLE (site-specific)
AGL (Atlanta Gas Light) territory — Competitive Gas Marketer Choice Customers in AGL territory buy the gas molecule from competing certified marketers on the open market; delivery through the regulated AGL pipe. PSC publishes a marketer pricing comparison. Site must be in AGL delivery territory. Enables gas-hedging structures alongside a battery-storage arbitrage. STABLE (site-specific)
Data-Center Cost-Shift Protection Rule (Georgia PSC, 2026) PSC rules mandate that new data-center customers do not shift infrastructure costs onto residential and other-class ratepayers. Applies to new large-load customer interconnection at Georgia Power. Impacts interconnect cost recovery. ACTIVE

Verify site qualification

Structure & entities

Site SPE formed, QROF certification filed, Georgia DOR data-center exemption pre-check (if applicable — track SB 410 status), SAM.gov entity registered.
