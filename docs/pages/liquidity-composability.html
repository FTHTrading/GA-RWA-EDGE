Liquidity & Composability · deRWA · Morpho · Grove Basin

Bankless liquidity for tokenized real-world asset holders. Wrap your Site SPE tokens as deRWA. Borrow stablecoins against them instantly. No credit check. No HELOC. No waiting weeks.

Traditional real estate investors who want cash without selling go to a bank for a HELOC or cash-out refinance — credit checks, underwriting, weeks of paperwork, high closing costs. Tokenized RWA holders bypass that entirely. Once a token is wrapped as deRWA and supplied to a Morpho lending market, borrowing USDC against it is a single on-chain transaction.

The RealT precedent — over 700 tokenized rental properties

RealT operates the largest tokenized single-family rental portfolio in the market — more than 700 U.S. properties available as fractional digital tokens. RealT's Real Market Maker (RMM) system was the first practical demonstration that a tokenized rental property could serve as instant DeFi loan collateral without any bank involvement. An investor deposits their yield-bearing property tokens into the RMM smart contract, and the contract instantly permits them to borrow stablecoins against the value of that real estate. No credit check. No underwriter. No wait.

GA-RWA-EDGE applies the same pattern to tokenized edge data-center Site SPE interests — with two important upgrades: (a) the underlying asset generates contracted AI hosting revenue (not just residential rental income), and (b) the Site SPE tokens are already ERC-3643 permissioned securities with counsel-opined transfer restrictions.

How it works — the 4-step deRWA lending flow

  1. Hold a Site SPE token · You are a verified-accredited investor who has subscribed to Site SPE I preferred equity tokens. The tokens sit in your BitGo, Anchorage, or Fireblocks wallet.
  2. Wrap as deRWA · You wrap your permissioned Site SPE token into a freely-transferable deRWA wrapper. The wrapper preserves the underlying compliance rules on the wrapped token but exposes an ERC-20-compatible interface for DeFi composability. Wrapping is a single on-chain transaction.
  3. Supply to Morpho or Grove Basin market · You supply your deRWA tokens to a dedicated lending market — either on Morpho (institutional prime-broker-style) or Grove Basin (Anemoy-affiliated warehouse credit). The market has been set up specifically for your asset class with an appropriate loan-to-value (LTV) and interest-rate model.
  4. Borrow stablecoins · You borrow USDC (or DAI, USDY, or another stablecoin) against your supplied collateral. The loan is over-collateralized — you can typically borrow up to 60-75% of the deRWA token's assessed value depending on the market's risk parameters. Interest accrues; you can repay any time; if the collateral value falls to a liquidation threshold, the market can partially liquidate your position.

Why this changes the investment case

Traditional Reg D private placements have historically been highly illiquid. You lock up capital for 5-10 years with no ability to access the cash until sale or maturity. The QROF structure amplifies this: you commit to a 10-year hold specifically to get the OZ tax benefit, and any sale before year 10 forfeits the 10-year tax-free appreciation.

deRWA composability creates a middle path. You hold the underlying token for the full 10 years — preserving the OZ tax benefit — but you can borrow against it whenever you need working capital, tuition, a home purchase, or another investment opportunity. You retain economic exposure to the site's cash flows and appreciation. You just monetize the collateral value of your position temporarily.

The tax preservation point Borrowing against your Site SPE token is not a taxable event. You don't sell, you don't recognize gain, you don't restart the OZ hold clock. Your basis stays the same. Your OZ benefits keep accruing while you use the borrowed cash for other purposes. This is the same tax logic that makes HELOCs attractive for traditional real estate — the tokenized version just happens instantly instead of taking six weeks.

The deRWA wrapper — how it preserves compliance

A Site SPE token is an ERC-3643 permissioned security. It cannot be transferred to a wallet that hasn't passed IdentityRegistry accreditation verification. Direct listing on a permissionless DeFi lending market would be blocked by the compliance modules.

The deRWA wrapper solves this. The wrapper is itself an ERC-20 token that represents ownership of the underlying Site SPE token held by the wrapper contract. The underlying token stays compliant — it never leaves the wrapper contract's compliance-approved address. The wrapper token is composable — it can move through DeFi lending markets, DEXs, and other on-chain venues.

Risks of the composability layer

Real risks — read carefully Bankless liquidity is powerful and also carries real risks. Investors should not use deRWA + Morpho / Grove Basin lending without understanding the following:
  1. Liquidation risk · If the deRWA collateral value falls to the market's liquidation threshold (LT), the market can partially or fully liquidate your position to recover the borrowed principal. This can result in loss of Site SPE token exposure. Maintain a healthy buffer between LTV and LT.
  2. Smart-contract risk · The deRWA wrapper, Morpho market, and Grove Basin facility are all smart contracts. Vulnerabilities can result in loss of both collateral and borrowed amounts. Only use audited contracts on chains with strong operator diligence.
  3. Oracle risk · The market relies on an on-chain oracle to price the deRWA collateral. Oracle manipulation or lag can trigger unfair liquidations.
  4. Interest-rate risk · Utilization-based interest-rate curves mean the borrow rate can spike if the market becomes heavily utilized. Model your carrying cost with a rate-stress scenario.
  5. Regulatory risk · Using tokenized securities as DeFi collateral is a live regulatory frontier. Sanctions, tax treatment, and disclosure obligations vary by jurisdiction. Consult tax and securities counsel before engaging.
  6. Illiquid underlying · If the underlying Site SPE tokens experience a covenant breach (DSCR trigger, cash trap) or a valuation event (revenue miss), the deRWA collateral value can fall sharply. Site SPE risks propagate through the wrapper.
