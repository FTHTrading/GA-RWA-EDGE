Why Metro Atlanta for AI compute infrastructure

Hyperscaler footprint

Atlanta is the Southeast's AI center of gravity

Metro Atlanta hosts data-center pipelines from Microsoft, Google, Meta, and Amazon. The QTS Metro Atlanta campus, Digital Realty ATL sites, and Switch Atlanta campus form an already-installed base of hyperscaler colocation. Every new AI compute site in Metro Atlanta prices into this ecosystem — power availability, fiber density, and hyperscaler-tenant demand are all above Southeast average.

Georgia Power carve-out

The 900 kW competitive-supply threshold

Under the Georgia Territorial Electric Service Act (O.C.G.A. § 46-3-1 et seq.), new premises drawing above 900 kW may choose their supplier at initial connection. Every serious edge or AI compute site clears the threshold. This unlocks competitive procurement, gas hedging, and blended-rate optimization that no residential-tariff site can access.

OZ 2.0 rural ring

The exurban Atlanta opportunity zone

Between Metro Atlanta core and rural North Georgia sits an exurban ring of Qualified Opportunity Zone tracts — Douglas County, Paulding County, Bartow County, Newton County, and Carroll County among them. OZ 2.0 rural sites qualify for the 30% basis step-up plus the standard 10-year federal cap-gains-to-zero mechanic. A hyperscaler-adjacent OZ compute site is a rare double-qualified asset.

Fiber and interconnect

Atlanta's fiber density is the second-best in the South

Georgia Tech, Emory, the CDC, and multiple Fortune 500 HQs made Atlanta a fiber-first city long before the AI wave. Peering exchanges (56 Marietta Street, Equinix AT-1/AT-2/AT-3, Digital Realty ATL1) provide interconnect topology that supports both AI training clusters and edge-inference deployment.
