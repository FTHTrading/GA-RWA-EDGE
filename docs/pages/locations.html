Site selection discipline — the 7-step screen

  1. PPA / power path first · locked PPA rate + assignability language + utility posture on novation. No power path, no deal.
  2. Zoning verification letter · written letter from county zoning official confirming data-center use is permitted, before any option agreement.
  3. Rural QROF tract test · pull tract ID from 2020 Census. Confirm no city / town > 50k population within tract and no urbanized-area contiguity. Verify against 2027 map when certified.
  4. Site control on option, not purchase · option agreement pre-Jan 1, 2027 to preserve OZ deployment discipline. Purchase closes at deal close.
  5. Fiber diligence · minimum 2 carrier options. Latency to Atlanta / Dallas / Charlotte AI-cloud PoPs.
  6. Water + climate · immersion needs modest but nonzero water. Ambient design temp per NOAA design-day data.
  7. Community engagement · show up at county EDC early with jobs + tax base story. This is how you convert "yes with letter" jurisdictions into "best."

Multi-site portfolio economics

Every additional site on the same rails compounds three ways:

  • Fixed cost amortization — legal, custody, and rail-configuration costs are largely fixed per fund vehicle. Second site adds ~$50k of incremental setup vs. $500k for site one.
  • Seasoned book graduation — after 6-12 months of clean payment history across 3-5 sites, the note book qualifies for Centrifuge V3 warehouse leverage at 70-90% advance rate on senior tranche.
  • Diversification — multiple sites reduce single-tenant concentration and single-PPA counterparty risk that Applied Digital / CoreWeave-style deals concentrate.
