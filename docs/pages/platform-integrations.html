Layer 05 · Funding Sources — three new sub-layers

The current Layer 05 lists traditional sources (senior debt, mezz, preferred equity, federal incentives). Adding three new RWA-native sub-layers unlocks materially cheaper capital for seasoned deals, investor-side liquidity, and yield-bearing treasury.

Layer 05.A · Institutional warehouse credit (seasoned book)

Once a Site SPE has 6-12 months of clean payment history, the note book graduates from initial 506(c) equity + senior bank paper into institutional warehouse facilities. This is the exact upstream move Grove made from a $50M CLO investment (Dec 2025) to a $500M warehouse position with Galaxy Digital (July 2026).

Bottom line

The capital.unykorn.ai/platform/ site has good bones — 5 named layers with real partners and a specific reference deal. What it's missing is the RWA-native infrastructure layer that has emerged rapidly in 2025-2026 (Grove Basin, Ondo OUSG-scale treasury, Morpho institutional isolated markets, Centrifuge V3 Anemoy tier). Adding those as sub-layers under Funding Sources (and firming up the Layer 04 TBD to Securitize Markets) transforms the platform from a well-scaffolded documentation site into one that shows the full institutional-grade RWA plumbing that unlocks materially cheaper capital.

None of these additions require new commitments. Every named counterparty above is already operating at scale with public disclosure. Adding them to the platform documentation is a documentation update, not a technology build. The actual integration work — deploying deRWA wrappers, launching Morpho markets, opening Ondo OUSG accounts — sequences over Q4 2026 through Q3 2027 alongside the Site SPE I close and M Helen stabilization.
