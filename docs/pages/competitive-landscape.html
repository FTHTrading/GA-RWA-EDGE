Competitive Landscape · RWA Infrastructure · July 2026

The RWA tokenization + institutional lending landscape has consolidated. Roughly 50 names matter. Here's who does what, at what scale, and where each sits relative to our stack.

Every entity below is either a partner (integration counterparty), a precedent (validates our category), a competitor (does something adjacent to our stack), or a venue (a place where our tokens or notes eventually trade). None of them do what GA-RWA-EDGE does end-to-end: PPA-anchored edge data-center site SPEs wrapped in QROF with contracted AI hosting revenue — but many operate the specific plumbing our stack integrates with.

Fresh precedent · Grove × Galaxy Digital · $500M warehouse (July 2026) Grove announced a $500M warehouse lending facility with Galaxy Digital: Grove is the warehouse lender providing USDS capital, Galaxy originates and services BTC/ETH-backed loans, with Anchorage Digital and BitGo as qualified custodians and Chronicle providing price feeds. Senior secured, 2-year max original terms, 20% cap on loans exceeding one year, 50% ETH concentration cap with 50% sub-cap on staked ETH. This is the reference precedent for our Site SPE-note-book → Grove Basin warehouse graduation path in Year 3-4.

Strategic assessment — three ways this facility validates our architecture

1 · Upstream capital position

Warehouse vs. secondary participation

Grove moved upstream from a $50M CLO investment in Galaxy paper (Dec 2025) to a $500M warehouse position — providing the foundational capital Galaxy uses to originate loans rather than buying secondary tranches. That's closer to origination and collateral.

Our trajectory: Site SPE notes start as 506(c) subordinate raises + senior bank paper. As they season, they graduate into institutional warehouse facilities — the same upstream trajectory Grove just demonstrated on the credit side.

2 · Strict eligibility mirrors our gating

Collateral standards + oracles + custody

Senior-secured first-priority interest, sub-2-year terms, continuous LTV monitoring via Chronicle oracles, top-tier qualified custody at BitGo and Anchorage.

Our stack mirrors this: ReserveProofAnchor anchors every hash, DSCRTrigger monitors coverage continuously, CustodyAdapter gates minting into BitGo Bank & Trust N.A. Every asset entering a Site SPE must meet transparent on-chain criteria before stablecoin liquidity can be routed.

3 · Non-custodial sovereignty

Grove Allocator = Unykorn playbook

Grove operates entirely via non-custodial vault systems (Grove Allocator) that maintain on-chain transparency without risking institutional balance sheets or taking direct custody of underlying collateral.

Direct validation: Unykorn earns technology fees (setup, SaaS, per-attestation) while never taking custody. Asset ownership stays in bankruptcy-remote SPEs. Distribution rights live in FTH Trading. Grove's approach is the same architectural stance — different asset class, same discipline. See Revenue model.

Where GA-RWA-EDGE sits in this landscape

Not doing

General-purpose RWA infrastructure

We don't compete with Securitize, Tokeny, or Polymesh on generic tokenization infrastructure. Those are horizontal plays. GA-RWA-EDGE is vertical: PPA-anchored edge compute + rural QROF specifically.

Not doing

Tokenized treasuries or T-bills

We don't compete with Ondo, BUIDL, or Superstate. We consume their products as SPE working capital via OUSG / USDY / BUIDL treasury holdings.

Doing

PPA-anchored edge data-center QROF issuance

No competitor operates this exact vertical. The closest peers (Applied Digital, IREN, Soluna) are public compute operators — none of them are QROF-wrapped or tokenized. The closest tokenization peers (Centrifuge, Maple) don't do QROF or edge data centers.

Integrating

The venue side (Grove, Morpho, Aave, Centrifuge)

We integrate as an asset originator into every institutional lending / warehouse venue that will accept our seasoned notes. This is where the Grove × Galaxy facility precedent matters most.

Bottom line

The RWA infrastructure space is crowded and getting more competitive every quarter. What matters for GA-RWA-EDGE is that no player in the landscape above is doing PPA-anchored edge compute with QROF equity distribution. The pieces we don't own (custody, tokenized treasuries, warehouse lending, DeFi venues) all have named institutional providers we integrate with, not compete against. The pieces we do own (LD Capital issuance layer, Unykorn technology rails, FTH Trading BD-track distribution, Asset SPE structure) are vertically-integrated to a specific asset class where none of the horizontal players play.

The Grove × Galaxy Digital $500M facility announced this month is validating in the exact way that matters: institutional-scale warehouse lending against tokenized real-world collateral is now a live, funded category. Our seasoned Site SPE note book graduates into that same category once we have 6-12 months of clean payment history. The precedent is set.
