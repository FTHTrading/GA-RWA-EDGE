Why this deal is different

Locked power

4.9¢/kWh fixed PPA

Below-market power under a fixed-rate contract as represented by the operator. Wholesale power in metro Southeast markets currently runs 6–9¢/kWh, versus the represented 4.9¢/kWh at Barak — the largest single cost-input advantage in compute infrastructure if verified. Georgia electricity remains regulated (Territorial Act large-load carve-out); AGL gas hedge availability is site-territory specific.

Subscription mechanics — 10 steps

  1. Request access to the accredited investor portal via the portal gate.
  2. Verify accreditation through Persona or Parallel Markets (Reg D 506(c) requires third-party verification — not self-certification).
  3. Review definitive PPM, subscription agreement, and investor questionnaire.
  4. Provide cap-gain tax lot information if using the 180-day OZ rollover.
  5. Sign subscription documents and complete KYC / AML.
  6. Wire subscription funds to bank / trust escrow (NOT to any platform wallet).
  7. Wallet mapping: your wallet is registered in IdentityRegistry with your verified accreditation claim.
  8. Closing: at deal close, escrow releases fiat to Site SPE and tokens mint atomically to your wallet.
  9. Custody: hold at BitGo Bank & Trust, Anchorage, Fireblocks, or compliant self-custody.
  10. Ongoing: quarterly distributions, annual K-1, on-chain-verifiable reports.

The four things that must be true for this deal to close

  1. Barak PPA diligence completes cleanly — term, assignability, entity authority, novation posture, and existing miner contracts all resolve as expected.
  2. Site's census tract is designated rural QROF — 2027 OZ 2.0 map certification confirms tract eligibility. Fallback: standard QOF (10% step-up).
  3. Pre-mainnet blockers clear — dual smart-contract audits, securities counsel opinion, BitGo custody live, BD-of-record executed.
  4. Senior debt term sheet is signed — $12M senior at target 60-70% LTV from a named data-center or USDA lender.

If any of these fails, the offering does not launch. Investor funds never leave escrow unless all four hold.
