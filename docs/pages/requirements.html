Client Requirements & Diligence Intake

What we need from you, and what you should demand from us.

For asset originators, sponsors, EPC contractors, senior lenders, and investors engaging FTH Trading / LD Capital / Unykorn on Georgia edge-DC and tokenized real-asset deals. Complete Part A before we can pass diligence; hold us to every item in Part B.

Which track are you on?

Track A

Asset originator / site owner

You control power (PPA or utility contract) and / or land / site. You want us to structure a deal on it — SPE formation, senior debt, QROF equity.

Skip to Part A · Sections A1-A5.

Track B

Accredited investor

You want to invest in a Site SPE. You need accreditation verification, PPM review, subscription mechanics.

Skip to Part C · Investor requirements.

Track C

Senior lender / debt provider

You want to underwrite senior debt against a Site SPE. You need SPE structure, PPA assignment, appraisals.

Skip to Part D · Lender requirements.

Track D

EPC / vendor / partner

You want to be evaluated as a builder or vendor partner. You need to be pre-qualified against our EPC scorecard.

Part A — Asset originator intake (Track A)

A1 · Entity & ownership

  • Formation documents (articles, operating agreement), EIN letter, good-standing certificate
  • Full beneficial-ownership chart to natural persons (UBOs at 25%+, control persons)
  • Officer / authorized-signer list; W-9/W-8 as applicable
  • Any existing SPE structure for the asset — or we form one (borrower is always a single-asset SPE)

A2 · The asset / site

  • Address, county, parcel ID(s), acreage; current title status (owned / under option / target)
  • OZ tract ID if known (we verify against 2027 designation cycle; rural status drives the 30% tier)
  • Existing zoning classification; any correspondence with the county
  • Written zoning verification letter required before capital moves
  • Environmental: Phase I ESA (if available), wetlands, floodplain knowledge, historic-district status

A3 · Power — the gating item

Incomplete power data stops intake — this is Priority 1 for us as it is for the waterfall
  • Executed PPA or binding load agreement (redacted acceptable at first review)
  • Delivery point (substation identifier, voltage, physical address)
  • Term length + rate structure (fixed / indexed / hybrid / escalators / demand charges)
  • Capacity firmness (firm vs curtailable) and any take-or-pay provisions
  • Exact assignability / novation language (this is Go/No-Go)
  • Counterparty (utility or generator) consent requirements
  • Environmental attributes ownership (who owns RECs, SRECs, carbon)
  • Termination triggers and cure periods
  • Any PSC review posture applicable

A4 · Offtake / revenue

  • Any existing hosting / offtake LOIs or term sheets
  • Historical operating financials (revenue, opex, DSCR history if operational)
  • For existing mining sites: current utilization, hashrate contracts, hardware inventory

A5 · Financial & operating

  • Historical or projected operating budget
  • Any existing debt or liens on the asset (title commitment or recent title search)
  • Local labor / community-benefit commitments (helpful for municipal support)

Nice-to-have (accelerates underwriting)

  • Third-party appraisal or independent engineer report
  • Walkthrough video / high-resolution site photos
  • Interconnection study or load-application status with utility
  • Any prior AI hosting / colo customer conversations

Part B — What you should demand from us (any track)

The 10 items that separate real platforms from marketing
  1. Show me the code — inspectable contracts, published mainnet addresses at deploy. Marketing sites describing nonexistent systems are the industry's #1 red flag.
  2. Who holds the keys — qualified custody, model named. Target: 2-of-3 with client holding one key; platform can never move funds alone.
  3. Where money sits pre-close — bank / trust escrow only, never a platform operating account. If someone asks you to wire subscription funds to a "platform wallet," walk away.
  4. Explicit fees — every fee a named bps or dollar line. Opaque spreads are unauditable fees.
  5. No promised returns — guaranteed yield, SBLC "monetization," or upfront release fees = walk away, from anyone.
  6. Licenses verified — issuers may sell their own paper (506(c)); anyone selling others' securities for compensation must be a registered BD. Check FINRA BrokerCheck at brokercheck.finra.org.
  7. Registry-backed ESG — a REC or carbon credit without a tracking-registry serial is a story, not an asset. Our contracts refuse to mint without one.
  8. Compliance as a gate — an unverified wallet must be structurally unable to receive tokens. Enforcement before movement, not review after.
  9. Reproducible math — waterfall distributions reconcile to an independent Python model to the cent. Ask for the reconciliation any period.
  10. Engineered downside — DSCR triggers, cash traps, loss order, and key-recovery answers should be crisp. If they aren't, the downside was never designed.

Part C — Accredited investor requirements (Track B)

C1 · Accreditation documentation

  • Government-issued ID (passport or state ID)
  • Third-party verification through Persona or Parallel Markets (Reg D 506(c) requires third-party — not self-certification)
  • For income accreditation: two most recent years of W-2 or tax returns
  • For net-worth accreditation: recent asset and liability statements, or CPA / attorney / broker-dealer letter

C2 · KYC / AML

  • Source of funds documentation
  • Sanctions screening (OFAC + international sanctions lists)
  • Politically Exposed Person (PEP) screening
  • Wallet address for token receipt (BitGo, Anchorage, Fireblocks, or self-custody with justification)

C3 · Tax

  • If using QROF cap-gain rollover: tax lot information for the source gain, dated within 180 days
  • W-9 or W-8BEN as applicable
  • Any state tax residency issues (state OZ conformity varies)

Part D — Senior lender requirements (Track C)

D1 · SPE structure

  • Bankruptcy-remote SPE operating agreement with separateness covenants
  • Independent director / manager appointment
  • SPE-only bank account and books
  • No other business, no cross-affiliate obligations

D2 · Asset diligence

  • Third-party appraisal (as-is + as-completed values)
  • Environmental Phase I (and Phase II if flagged)
  • Title commitment + survey
  • PPA assignment / novation documentation (fully executed)
  • Executed EPC contract (fixed-price preferred)

D3 · Revenue underwriting

  • Contracted hosting revenue LOIs or executed agreements
  • DSCR projection tested NET of PPA demand charges
  • Sensitivity analysis (base / downside / stress cases)
  • Sources & uses statement

D4 · Legal

  • OZ counsel confirmation of QROF / QOF eligibility
  • Securities counsel Rule 144 opinion (if secondary sale is contemplated)
  • Consent to collateral assignment from PPA counterparty utility
  • Lender step-in rights in EPC contract

Part E — EPC / vendor partner qualification (Track D)

E1 · Company diligence

  • Corporate financials (audited if available)
  • Recent project references at 1-20 MW modular / liquid-immersion scale
  • Track record on schedule + budget adherence
  • Insurance coverage (general liability, E&O, workers comp)

E2 · Modular EPC 12-clause scorecard

See the Modular EPC 12-Clause Review PDF for our scorecard. Your term sheet or standard EPC contract will be scored A through L. Term sheets that fail three or more clauses are returned; passing ones move to counsel for detailed negotiation. Non-negotiable items:

  • Fixed price (lump sum)
  • Quantitative performance guarantees (PUE, uptime, IT load capacity)
  • FAT / SAT / IST staged acceptance gates
  • Senior-lender step-in rights + full assignability

Part F — Data-room index (Track A + C)

For any live deal we build a data room organized as follows. Your intake should be delivered into these folders where you have documents ready:

data-room/
├── 01_entity/              org chart · formation docs · beneficial ownership · EIN / good-standing
├── 02_power/               executed PPA · interconnect agreement · rate schedules · assignment language
├── 03_site/                title / option · zoning verification letter · survey · Phase I ESA
├── 04_offtake/             hosting LOIs · offtake term sheets · customer intro emails
├── 05_epc/                 EPC term sheets · vendor quotes · milestone schedules · warranty terms
├── 06_financials/          historical opex / revenue · budget · debt schedule
├── 07_regulatory/          PSC filings · utility correspondence · county EDC letters
├── 08_environmental/       REC / SREC ownership · carbon attribute allocations · emissions attestations
├── 09_appraisals/          independent engineer reports · third-party appraisals
├── 10_counsel/             OZ opinion · securities opinion · title commitment · legal SPE package
└── 11_attestations/        on-chain hashes for every doc above via ReserveProofAnchor
Every document in the data room ends up hash-anchored on-chain The last folder (11_attestations) is where every document above gets its SHA-256 hash written to ReserveProofAnchor with the corresponding docType. This is what lets us prove — years later — that a specific version of a specific document existed at a specific time and was the version relied on by the underwriting.

Timeline expectation

From complete Part A intake to signed senior debt term sheet: 4-6 weeks under normal conditions with clean PPA diligence. From term sheet to close: 6-8 weeks including QROF fund formation, PPM finalization, investor onboarding, and escrow funding. Total intake → close: 10-14 weeks for a well-prepared originator.

Incomplete intake extends the timeline linearly. Missing the PPA assignment language, entity authority, or tract ID typically adds 2-4 weeks each while we chase resolution.
