The power position — one PPA, arbitraged between loads

A contracted, below-market power position is the scarce asset. Across this sector the binding constraint is power and interconnect, not capital — so a locked 7-year rate is the moat, not a line item.

Same power, two ways to monetize

The floor

Bitcoin — monetize every MW today

  • No tenant needed — point power at hashrate, earn from day one
  • Fully interruptible — curtail on price spikes; earn demand-response / grid credits
  • Hashprice-dependent — lower $/MW
  • Role: never leave an idle MW unmonetized
The premium

AI / HPC colocation — capture more per MW

  • Much higher $/MW — contracted colocation NOI
  • Needs an anchor tenant + firmer power & cooling
  • Tenant-gated — the honest constraint
  • Role: convert MW from Bitcoin as tenants sign
The switch You're not betting on one load — the PPA lets you arbitrage between them. Bitcoin monetizes every megawatt today and curtails when power is worth more elsewhere; AI captures the premium as tenants land. Power in, best margin out — the contract is what makes the option yours.

Risk register — the 8 items that could kill the deal

  1. PPA not cleanly assignable — diligence Go/No-Go items 1, 6, 12. Utility blocks assignment or restricts consent unreasonably → walk away.
  2. Rural QROF tract fails 2027 certification — fallback to standard QOF (10% step-up). Deal still works with modestly reduced tax benefit.
  3. Existing 721 miner contracts non-terminable — buyout cost exceeds $2M provision, requires equity increase.
  4. Hosting counterparty concentration — single-tenant AI hosting deals concentrate credit risk. Underwriting requires multi-tenant target.
  5. Cost overrun on immersion retrofit — fixed-price EPC + 10-15% contingency in stack. Sensitivity: every $2M/MW overrun adds ~$8-10M equity.
  6. GPU depreciation curve — chips are harder collateral than land + power. Structure equipment debt with matching amortization.
  7. DSCR breach — Priority 1 utility payment sweeps before senior debt. If hosting revenue falls > 65%, DSCR triggers cash trap; > 75% triggers turbo amort.
  8. Local ordinance change — county EDC engagement + written zoning verification letter before any capital moves.

Governance and voting

Preferred token holders are non-voting except for material transactions requiring holder consent:

  • Sale of substantially all Site SPE assets
  • Dissolution or bankruptcy filing
  • Amendment of LLC agreement in a manner adverse to preferred holders
  • Modification of PPA rate or term (would materially affect DSCR)
  • Change of servicer / QROF administrator

All routine operational decisions (hosting contract signing, capex within budget, refinance decisions) are made by the Sponsor Manager under the delegated authority in the LLC operating agreement.
