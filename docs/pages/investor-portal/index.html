Verification workflow

  1. Register interest above with basic contact information.
  2. Persona / Parallel Markets verification — third-party accreditation verification (income, net worth, entity type, or license-holder pathway).
  3. KYC / AML — government-issued ID, source of funds, sanctions screening.
  4. Portal access granted — access to deal book, PPM, and live model.
  5. Sponsor Q&A — schedule call with deal team.
  6. Subscription (when offering opens) — execute Subscription Agreement, provide tax lot for QROF rollover, wire funds to bank / trust escrow.
  7. Closing — atomic token mint on release from escrow.
  8. Ongoing — quarterly distributions + annual K-1 + on-chain-verifiable reports.
