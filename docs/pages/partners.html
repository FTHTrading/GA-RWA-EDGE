Broker-Dealer path · three stages

Stage 1

BD-of-record partnership

Dalmore-class or Securitize Markets pattern. A registered broker-dealer papers the third-party placement of Site SPE preferred equity while FTH refers investors and handles all non-securities work.

Timing: Q3-Q4 2026 execution.

Stage 2

Own FINRA BD

FTH files Form NMA. 12-18 months, $250-750k all-in. FTH becomes its own FINRA-member BD, capturing placement economics directly.

Timing: filed Q4 2026, approval mid-2027.

Stage 3

LDX-as-ATS

FTH files Form ATS. LDX venue registers as Alternative Trading System under Reg ATS. Secondary trading of Site SPE tokens, listing fees, and trading bps flow through LDX.

Timing: late 2027 onward.
