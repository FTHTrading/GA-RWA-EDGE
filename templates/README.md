## Non-negotiable rules

- 🟥 **No template is legal advice.** Every executed deal document must be reviewed by counsel with jurisdictional expertise.
- 🟥 **PPA assignment ≠ novation.** Only novation releases the original counterparty from further obligation. Senior lenders typically demand novation or a true-sale opinion.
- 🟥 **506(c) requires verified accreditation.** Persona / Parallel Markets provides the verification; the PPM must not permit self-certified accreditation.
- 🟥 **QOF 90% asset test runs semiannually forever.** The CPA administrator engagement must include the ongoing calendar, not just formation.
